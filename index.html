Materials

All FDM, 0.4 mm nozzle standard (0.2 mm available for fine detail). Layer heights 0.08–0.28 mm.

MaterialBest forNotes
PLA / PLA+Display pieces, prototypes, indoor partsCheapest, sharpest detail. Softens in an Arizona car.
PETGFunctional parts, outdoor, bracketsTougher, heat-resistant to ~75 °C. Our default for “it has to work.”
TPU (95A)Flexible: bumpers, gaskets, antenna mountsRubber-like. Great for crash-tolerant parts.
ASASun-exposed, high heatUV-stable. Right choice for anything living outside in AZ.
PA-CF / PETG-CFStiff structural partsCarbon-fiber filled. Ask — priced per job.
