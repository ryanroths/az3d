About

Small shop. Real parts.

AZ3D is run by Ryan out of Mesa, Arizona. Day job in logistics, nights and weekends flying FPV whoops and printing the parts that keep them in the air.

The printer is a Bambu Lab X1 Carbon with AMS — enclosed, fast, and multi-material. It handles PLA through carbon-fiber nylon and holds tolerances well enough for press-fit parts.

Every job gets sliced by hand, not auto-quoted by a robot. If your model has a problem — thin walls, a bad overhang, unprintable geometry — you hear about it before you pay, not after.
